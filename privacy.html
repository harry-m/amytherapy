It follows the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003.

Why I can use your information

The law says I need a reason, called a lawful basis, to hold and use your information. Mine depend on where we are in working together.

  • When you first get in touch, and while we are working together: it is needed so I can reply to you, take the steps you ask for before we start, and carry out our agreement to work together.
  • Information about your health and wellbeing is more sensitive. I ask for your consent to use it at the start of our work. You can withdraw your consent at any time, although that may mean I can no longer work with you.
  • After our work ends: I have a legitimate interest in keeping records so that I can respond to any complaint or legal claim.

How I use your information

When you first get in touch

I collect what I need to reply to you: your name, email address, phone number and the message you send. [Add anything else you record at this stage.]

If we do not go ahead, I will delete your details within one month, or sooner if you ask me to.

While we are working together

What you tell me is confidential. I will only share it without your permission if there is a serious risk of harm to you or someone else, or I am required to by law. Where I can, I will speak to you first, unless doing so would put someone at risk.

I am bound by the ethical guidance of BACP and UKCP.

  • My session notes are handwritten, have all identifying information removed, and are kept locally on a password-protected device.
  • Text messages are kept for no longer than one month. Anything relevant to our work is recorded in my notes.
  • Emails are deleted after no more than one month. Anything relevant to our work is recorded in my notes.
  • I discuss my work with a supervisor, as professional standards require. The anonymity of my clients is protected in those discussions and I do not share any identifying data.

After our work ends

I keep my records for 7 years after our last contact, and then destroy them securely. If you ask me to delete them sooner, I will consider that request.

Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • give you a copy of the information I hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete your information or limit how I use it
  • stop using your information in a particular way, if you object to it

Where I use your information because you consented, you can withdraw that consent at any time.

To make a request, email amy@amymetcalfetherapy.com. I may not always be able to do what you ask, for example where I need to keep records for legal reasons, but I will always explain why. You can read more about your rights on the ICO’s website.

If you are unhappy

Please tell me first so that I can put things right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

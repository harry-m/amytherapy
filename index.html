Therapy

Therapy offers a space where we can explore the edges of what we perceive about ourselves and our world. I believe that exploration always leads to change, whether that’s feeling differently about what is or the courage to do something differently.

My aim is to work together with you to understand you and your life as you see and feel it in the present moment. I offer a warm, compassionate and trauma-informed space which is sex positive and LGBTQIA+ affirming, and somewhere you can be exactly as you are.

My training is integrative and I draw on a variety of clinical theory and therapeutic approaches, but I will work with you to discover what of that is useful for you.

What I can help with

I work with people navigating a wide range of experiences including:

  • Anxiety
  • Low mood
  • Big life changes
  • Trauma
  • Complex trauma
  • Relationships
  • Burnout and overwhelm
  • Parenting
  • GSRD (gender, sexual and relationship diversity), including polyamory and ethical non-monogamy (ENM)

FAQs

Fees

My fee is £80 for a 50 minute session.

I can sometimes offer concessionary rates if you cannot afford my usual fee. Please reach out if you would like to discuss this.

Location

I see clients in person on Upper Street, N1.

Can I have an initial consultation?

It is important that you can get a feel for what it would be like to work together, and ask any questions.

  • I offer a free 15 minute phone consultation to introduce myself, hear what brings you to therapy and answer any initial questions.
  • I also offer an initial 50 minute session at £60, so you can get a feel for what it is like to be in the room together before you commit to a longer set of sessions.
How often will we meet for therapy?

I recommend we meet weekly at an agreed time.

How long does therapy go on for?

I am open to working short term or long term. We can tailor the work to meet your needs. Sometimes therapy can take time, but you are ultimately in charge of when you would like to end.
